How we work

Agents do the work. People make the decisions.

Automation earns trust when you can see what it did, why, and who approved it. Everything we build runs on that rule.

The process

Every change takes the same path

Each feature starts as a plan: what it should do and how everyone will know it works. Our agents check the plan before anyone approves it, so gaps surface on day one rather than in testing.

Once a person approves it, agents build it in your own repository, apart from what is live, and test it against what was agreed.

You try the result, and you decide when it reaches your customers. Agents never decide that for you.

Principles

What we hold ourselves to

Evidence on every conclusion

When an agent concludes something, it shows what it read: the files, the posts, the records. A finding without sources is dropped, not shown.

Your data stays yours

Each client's data is kept apart from every other client's, and credentials never reach an agent. Agents use your systems through access you grant and can withdraw.

A person owns every decision

Anything that reaches a customer, changes money or affects a person's job is decided by a named person, with the reason recorded.

On the record

See how every change got there

Every decision and every change is written to an audit log as it happens. For anything in production you can see who approved it and what the agents produced.

Your tools

It runs on your systems

The work lands in your own repository, your own cloud and your own accounts. You control when each new feature is switched on, and can switch it off again.
