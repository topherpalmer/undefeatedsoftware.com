Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026. This policy explains what Undefeated Software collects when you use our website and our products (Agent Foundry, Blitz and Draft Board), why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Undefeated Software ("we", "us") builds and runs AI-assisted software. Our customers are organizations; the people who use our products do so on behalf of an organization that has an account with us. For information our customers put into our products, we act on the customer's instructions. Questions about this policy go to hello@undefeatedsoftware.com.

What we collect

Account information. Your name, email address and profile picture when you sign in with Google or with a code sent to your email, the organization you belong to, and your role in it.

Billing information. Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive the billing contact, the last four digits and expiry of your card, and payment status. We never see or store full card numbers.

Content you give us. What you put into our products: feature descriptions, code and repositories you connect, CRM records, media files, brand voice, drafts and posts, and messages to our agents.

Connected social accounts. When you connect LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X or YouTube to Blitz, the platform gives us an access token and basic information about the account or page you chose (its name, ID and picture). With your permission we use it to publish posts you approve, and to read the posts, comments and engagement figures for those accounts so we can show results and suggest replies. We only request the permissions these features need.

Usage and technical information. Sign-in events, actions you take in our products, error logs, IP address and browser type, kept to run, secure and improve the service. Our website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies; our products use cookies or local storage only to keep you signed in and remember simple preferences.

How we use it

To provide the products you ask for; to have AI agents draft, build and analyze on your behalf; to publish content you approve to the accounts you connected; to bill you; to secure our systems and prevent abuse; to support you; and to tell you about changes to the service. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

AI processing

Our agents send the content needed for a task to AI model providers (such as Anthropic and xAI) to generate drafts, code, images and analysis. These providers process it to return a result to us. A person on your team reviews and approves what is published or shipped.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run parts of our service for us, under contracts that limit their use to that purpose: Google Cloud (hosting and storage), Anthropic and xAI (AI models), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), and GitHub (code you choose to connect). The social platforms you connect receive the posts and replies you approve. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your organization's account is active. Access tokens for a social account are deleted when you disconnect it. When an account is closed, we delete or anonymize its information within 30 days, except billing records and logs we must keep for legal, tax or security reasons.

Deleting your data

To remove a connected social account and its access token, disconnect it on the Channels page in Blitz; you can also remove our app from the platform's own settings (for example LinkedIn: Settings, Data privacy, Permitted services; Facebook and Instagram: Settings, Apps and websites). To delete your account and the information associated with it, email hello@undefeatedsoftware.com with the subject "Delete my data" from the address you sign in with. We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict some uses. Email us to make a request; we will respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity. If your organization manages your account, we may refer the request to it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Secrets such as access tokens are kept in a managed secret store, access is limited to the people and systems that need it, and every customer's data is kept separate from every other customer's.

International transfers

We are based in the United States and process information there. When we receive information from other countries, we protect it as this policy describes and use the safeguards the law requires.

Children

Our products are for businesses and are not directed to anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell account owners by email before the change takes effect.
