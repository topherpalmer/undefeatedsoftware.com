The brief, the spec, the viability co-sign, the design, acceptance and release.

An agentic go-to-market console for a B2B software company: daily trends, posts drafted from the company's own media, and every post approved before it goes out.

What the agents do
Find each day's trends per platform with their sources, draft 10 to 20 posts in the client's voice, check claims, and plan next week's shots.
Where people decide
Which posts go out, on which channels and when; who is invited to the console.
Built with
Invitation-only Google sign-in, per-client data, channel connections the client controls.
