How we work

Agents work between the gates. People decide at them.

Automation earns trust when you can see what it did, why, and who approved it. Everything we build runs on that rule.

The gates

Every change takes the same path

A feature starts as a short brief and a spec. An agent reviews the spec before anyone signs it, then scores how buildable it is against your code. An engineer co-signs that score.

A design agent lays out the flows and states, and a person signs the design off. Only then does a builder agent write code, on its own branch, behind a feature flag. A QA agent runs the tests and checks every acceptance criterion with the flag on and off.

A person accepts the result and decides when it is released. Agents never move work past a gate themselves.

Principles

What we hold ourselves to

Evidence on every conclusion

When an agent concludes something, it shows what it read: the files, the posts, the records. A finding without sources is dropped, not shown.

Your data stays yours

Each client's data is kept apart from every other client's, and credentials never reach an agent. Agents use your systems through access you grant and can withdraw.

A person owns every decision

Anything that reaches a customer, changes money or affects a person's job is decided by a named person, with the reason recorded.

On the record

See how every change got there

Every gate decision, every agent run and its cost is written to an audit log as it happens. For any change in production you can see who approved it, what the agents produced and how long it waited at each step.

Your tools

It runs on your systems

The work lands in your own repository, your own cloud and your own accounts. Features ship behind flags you control, and you can switch any of them off without a deploy.
